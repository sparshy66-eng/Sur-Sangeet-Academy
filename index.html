About

Learn Dance Online, At Your Own Pace

Sur Sangeet Online Dance Academy is dedicated to teaching Kathak, Semi Classical, Bollywood Kathak, and Bollywood dance through live, personalized online classes.

We welcome students of all age groups, from young beginners to adults, and offer flexible batch timings so you can learn at a time that suits you — from anywhere in the world.

Every class is conducted live online with individual attention, helping students build technique, expression, and confidence, whether they are just starting out or looking to refine their craft.
